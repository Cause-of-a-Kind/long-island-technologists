"What is your AI strategy?" is a silly question. That is like asking what your electricity strategy is.

Patrick Gilbert, CEO of AdVenture Media and author of *Never Always, Never Never: Strategic Marketing in an AI World*, joined Long Island Technologists at the rock climbing gym where Mike and I met over 20 years ago. He did not come to sell anyone on the hype. He came to tell a room full of builders that someone is actively trying to put them out of business right now, today, and the only reasonable response is to do it to yourself first.

Patrick spent the last 18 months tearing his own agency down to the studs. He talked about the client who fired him that same morning for a cheaper AI powered competitor, why it did not matter that the competitor's work was full of hallucinated nonsense, and how most companies are still trying to screw a light bulb into a factory instead of rebuilding the factory around the current.

We covered the light bulb versus electricity analogy, why internal efficiency is the boring half of the opportunity, how his team runs a hackathon to turn AI skeptics into builders overnight, the Blue Ocean escape hatch out of a commoditized market, and the Midjourney story that explains exactly where human taste still wins.

If you are still on the sidelines, you are earlier than you think. That is good news.
